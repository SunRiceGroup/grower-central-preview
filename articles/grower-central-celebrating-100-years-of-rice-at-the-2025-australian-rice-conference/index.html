Celebrating 100 years of rice at the 2025 Australian Rice Conference

This August, the rice industry will come together in Leeton for the much-anticipated Australian Rice Conference, a standout event on the grower calendar. Held from August 6–8, the 2025 conference is especially significant as it marks 100 years of rice, honouring the resilience, innovation, and legacy of the pioneers who shaped the industry.

Hosted bi-annually by the Ricegrowers’ Association of Australia (RGA) in partnership with the SunRice Group, AgriFutures Australia, and key sponsors, the conference brings together a broad cross-section of the industry; growers, researchers, service providers, marketers, food businesses, and exporters for connection, collaboration, and celebration.

This year’s program includes a trade exhibition, interactive sessions, and insights from leading voices in agriculture, agronomy, and water efficiency. The event also fosters important discussions on the future of rice growing in Australia, highlighting sustainable practices and strategies to remain competitive in a global market.

The Gala Dinner will be a major highlight, offering an evening of networking, storytelling, and reflection on a century of progress in Australian rice.
